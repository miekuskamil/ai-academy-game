# Neuron — a guide for you

Hi! This is a little course about how AI really works. Not the boring kind where
someone just tells you things — you get to build stuff, break stuff on purpose,
and figure things out yourself.

Here is everything you need to know to start.

## What is this?

AI is in loads of things now — phones, games, homework helpers — and almost
nobody gets told how it actually works inside. You are about to find out
properly, by doing it yourself.

You will teach a machine to spot patterns, feed it good and bad examples, look
inside how it reads words, break it on purpose to see what goes wrong, and only
*then* learn how to talk to it well.

Along the way you help **Iskra**, who is an AI that is not very good yet. That is
the whole point — you learn by spotting her mistakes and fixing them.

## How it works

There are **six worlds** and **twenty lessons**. Take as long as you like —
nothing is timed, and nothing is ever taken away from you.

Each lesson goes like this:

1. **Watch** — a little comic, usually with Iskra getting something wrong.
2. **Try** — have a go yourself in a hands-on bit.
3. **Learn** — the idea explained, plus the real words grown-ups use for it.
4. **Recap** — the important bits, short and clear.
5. **Test** — a few questions. Get them wrong as many times as you like; it never
   counts against you.
6. **Done** — another comic, and you earn a piece of your machine.

## The Machine

Here is the fun secret. Every lesson you finish earns you **one piece of a
mystery machine**. You do not know what it is yet — that is on purpose!

Finishing a lesson gives you a piece. Then you go to the **Machine** tab and
**place the piece yourself** — tap the piece, then tap its glowing slot, and it
clicks in. As you add more pieces, little hints appear about what you are
building.

When you have earned and placed all twenty pieces, the machine opens up — and you
find out what it was the whole time. No spoilers. You have to build it.

## Getting stuck is fine

Really. Getting a question wrong is not a problem here — it is how you learn.
Iskra gets things wrong all the time, and helping her is your job.

- You can retry any question. It never lowers your score.
- You can go back and read anything again.
- There are hints if you want them.
- Nobody is watching, there is no timer, and there are no leaderboards.

If you get everything right with no hints, you earn a **star** — but you never
*need* one to keep going.

## Where things are

- **Map** — your home. All six worlds and where you are up to.
- **Lesson** — jumps you back into what you are doing.
- **Machine** — your mystery build. Place your pieces here.
- **Iskra** — your AI friend, and the badges you have earned.
- **Grown-ups** — for a parent. Settings, and saving your progress to a file.

## A note on saving

Your progress saves by itself, on this device, every time you finish something.
You do not have to do anything.

If you ever want to move to a different tablet or phone, ask a grown-up — there is
a **Save** button in the Grown-ups area that makes a file you can carry over.

---

That is it. Go to the Map and press **Start here**. Have fun, and do not worry
about getting things wrong — that is the best way to learn.
